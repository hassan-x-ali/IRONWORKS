FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Month-to-month billing, no contracts. Cancel from your account dashboard with no fees.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Start a free trial and train for one week before committing to a plan.

What are your hours?

Mon–Fri 5am–11pm, Sat–Sun 8am–8pm. Pro and Elite members get 24/7 key-fob access.

Is it beginner-friendly?

Absolutely. Our Beginners program teaches the fundamentals safely with expert coaching.

Are there women-only hours?

Yes. Dedicated Women-Only Hours run in a supportive, reserved space.

Do you offer a student discount?

Yes. Valid student ID gets discounted pricing. Bring your .edu email or student card.
